Game data deletion

Under GDPR and the equivalent laws elsewhere you can ask us to delete the data one of our games holds about you.

How it works

Send the game name together with the Player ID shown on that game's settings screen. We verify the account is yours, delete the data, and email a confirmation to the address you give us. There is no retention offer and no second form.

If you cannot find your Player ID, or the request is about one of our apps rather than a game, email support@vestar.digital and we will handle it from there.
